3-Yr Net Present Value NPV: The present value of all future realized net cash flows minus initial CapEx, discounted at the enterprise Hurdle Rate. Must be > $0 to create shareholder value.
--
Return on Capital (ROIC) ROIC: Net annual operating cash flow generated divided by upfront invested capital. Must exceed the Hurdle Rate / WACC.
--
Payback Period Payback Period: The time (in years) required for cumulative realized hard cash inflows to fully recover the initial upfront CapEx outlay.
