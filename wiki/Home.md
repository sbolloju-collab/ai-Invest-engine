## Overview

This tool helps enterprise teams make data-driven decisions on AI investments by evaluating:

✅ **Capital Requirements** - Calculate total upfront costs (CapEx) and recurring costs (OpEx)  
✅ **Value Capture** - Quantify hard cash benefits from productivity improvements  
✅ **Build vs. Buy** - Compare custom in-house solutions vs. commercial SaaS  
✅ **Financial Returns** - Analyze NPV, ROIC, and payback horizons  
✅ **Risk Assessment** - Stress-test assumptions across different scenarios  

## Key Features

- **5-Step Guided Wizard** - Navigate through investment evaluation systematically
- **Automated Financial Calculations** - Real-time NPV, ROIC, and payback analysis
- **Build vs. Buy Comparison** - Side-by-side evaluation of in-house vs. commercial options
- **Sensitivity Analysis** - 2-way stress testing on hurdle rates and realization rates
- **Export Formats** - Save as JSON, CSV, or generate a professional PDF report
- **Mobile-Optimized** - Touch-friendly interface with 44px minimum touch targets
- **Auto-Save** - Your progress is saved automatically
